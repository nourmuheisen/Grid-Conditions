# Assumptions and data-gap log

Snapshot: 2026-10-15

## Assumptions and analytical boundaries

- **A01** — Selected locations illustrate different evidence types; selection does not establish that they are the most promising Dutch projects.
- **A02** — Scenario options are hypothetical counterfactuals for existing locations or a future expansion, not existing offers.
- **A03** — No actual financial, capacity, emissions or time-to-power outcomes are calculated.
- **A04** — Source-confirmed means the source makes the statement; owner statements are not independent engineering verification.
- **A05** — Unknown means not established in the reviewed source set, not absent in reality.
- **A06** — Editable scenario notes are user assumptions only and never alter the evidence record.
- **A07** — A zero event energy gap would not by itself establish net system benefit; rebound, recharge, fuel and location must also be tested.
- **A08** — National expansion uses purposive geographic and evidence-type coverage, not a census. Eight provinces are represented; missing provinces do not imply no data centres.

## Conflicts and non-equivalent evidence

- W06: preserve 2023 / 2024 Winschoten construction milestones (S04–S06).
- E08: retain both 2024 expected cable delivery windows (S21/S22); completion unverified.
- E04: unnamed 380 kV proposal is not assigned to Google (S15); do not merge with 220 kV reporting.
- P02: Google Westpoort to Vierverlaten is a cross-source project match, requiring confirmation (S19/S20).
- Eemshaven municipality is a locality inference, not a cadastral verification (S04/S15).
- N03: current hydrogen technology listing and historical planned 500 kW rating are not equivalent to a current dispatchable capacity.
- Export headroom (S13) and consumption constraints (S11/S12) concern different directions and potentially different network levels.
- D01: AMS11 owner page and industry report describe different opening times / phases (S25/S26); do not silently select a status.
- H04: Bolderhoek revised findings give a later local reinforcement expectation than earlier text on the same page (S39). No PDC2 attribution.
- National sample: regional statements S35–S39 are not facility contracts; Zestienhoven is not assigned Rotterdam-port congestion.
- Solar or air cooling does not establish demand flexibility. Neither an announcement nor an activity notification confirms commissioning or routine-operation permission.
