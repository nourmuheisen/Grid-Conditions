Scope and selection

This is a national exploratory sample of 12 documented cases across eight provinces, not an exhaustive inventory. Owner sources establish real cases; grid operators supply separately labelled context. DDA's map is a discovery resource, not a verified development pipeline. [S02]

Inclusion required identifiable owner evidence and a useful question for investigation. Operator concentration and mixed building/campus boundaries limit generalisation. No cases were added solely to fill geographic gaps. Provinces absent from this sample are not assumed to lack data centres.

Existing operations, announced expansions and phased delivery are not interchangeable opportunities. The category filter is an analyst lens, not a recommendation.

Why there is no numerical score

Site load, curtailment windows, dispatch permissions, commercial terms and network counterfactuals are missing. No savings, secured capacity, emissions reduction or time-to-power estimate is calculated. Scenario notes remain user assumptions, never verified evidence.

Published MW values retain their stated basis. Installed electrical capacity, IT capacity, grid voltage, available import capacity and stored energy are not interchangeable.
